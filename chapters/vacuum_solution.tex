In this chapter, we study the vacuum solution to the Einstein field equations. This is the case in which no gravitational fields are present. The only spherically symmetric vacuum solution is called the Schwarzschild metric

\section{Mathematical Formulation}

The vacuum Einstein field equation is given by
\begin{align}
    G_{\mu \nu} &= 0\\
    R_{\mu \nu} - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu \nu}R &= 0
\end{align}
The Schwarzschild metric is defined by
\begin{align}
    ds^2 *= -(1 - \frac{2GM}{r})dt^2 + (1 - \frac{2GM}{r})^{-1} dr^2 + r^2 d\Omega^2
\end{align}
Here we have $d\Omega^2$ is the metric on the sphere
\begin{align}
    d\Omega^2 &= d\theta^2 + \sin^2 \theta d\phi^2
\end{align}
We can use this metric to solve for the Christoffel symbols.

\begin{align}
\Gamma^t_{tr} &= \partial_r \alpha \\
\Gamma^\theta_{r\theta} &= \frac{1}{r} \\
\Gamma^r_{\phi\phi} &= -re^{-2\beta}\sin^2 \theta \\
\Gamma^r_{tt} &= e^{2(\alpha - \beta)} \partial_r \alpha \\
\Gamma^r_{\theta\theta} &= -re^{-2\beta} \\
\Gamma^\theta_{\phi \phi} &= -\sin \theta \cos \theta \\
\Gamma^r_{rr} &= \partial_r \beta \\
\Gamma^\phi_{r\phi} &= \frac{1}{r} \\
\Gamma^\phi_{\theta \phi} &= \frac{\cos \theta}{\sin \theta}
\end{align}
