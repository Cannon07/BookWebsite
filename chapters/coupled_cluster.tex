\chapter{Coupled Cluster Methods}
\label{chap:coupled_cluster}
\noindent\rule{11cm}{0.4pt} \\
\textbf{Prerequisites:} \autoref{chap:quantum_mechanics}, \autoref{chap:molecular_hamiltonian}  \\
\textbf{Difficulty Level:} ** \\
\noindent\rule{11cm}{0.4pt}
\vspace{5mm}

Coupled cluster methods are a class of numerical methods used to model many-body quantum mechanical systems. Coupled cluster techniques are traditionally held up as the "gold standard" for modeling complex quantum mechanical systems. The accuracy of couple cluster methods comes at the cost of high computational cost.
