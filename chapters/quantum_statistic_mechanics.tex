\chapter{Quantum Statistical Mechanics}

\label{chap:quantum_statistical_mechanics}
\noindent\rule{11cm}{0.4pt} \\
\textbf{Prerequisites:}  \autoref{chap:schrodinger} \\
\textbf{Difficulty Level:} *** \\
\noindent\rule{11cm}{0.4pt}
\vspace{5mm}

The core mathematical object of statistical mechanics is the partition function. To introduce a theory of quantum statistical mechanics, we will consequently find it useful to introduce the following quantum partition function
\begin{align}
    Z_Q &= \Tr e^{-\beta H} &= \Tr U(\tau &= \beta \hbar)
\end{align}
Here $H$ is the usual Hamiltonian and $U$ is the imaginary time evolution operator
\begin{align}
    U(\tau) &= e^{-\frac{\tau}{\hbar}H}
\end{align}
