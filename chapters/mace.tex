In this chapter, we learn about higher order equivariant message passing neural networks that pass more than two body messages.

\section{A Brief Review of Message Passing}

The state of node $i$ in layer $t$ of the MPNN is given by

\begin{align}
\sigma_i^{(t)} &= (r_i, z_i, h_i^{(t)})
\end{align}

The energy is given by
\begin{align}
E_i &= \sum_{t=1}^T \mathcal{R}_t(\sigma_i^{(t)})
\end{align}

ACE introduced higher order local descriptors. %\textcolor{red}{TODO: Go back and explain how the ACE descriptors subsume SOAP}.
Multi-ACE is an extension of ACE using equivariant MPNNs.

\section{The MACE Architecture}

Expand the messages using a hierarchical body ordered expansion

\begin{align}
m_i^{(t)} &= \sum_j u_1(\sigma_i^{(t)}, \sigma_j^{(t)}) +  \sum_{j_1, j_2}^{(t)} u_2(\sigma_i^{(t)}, \sigma_{j_1}^{(t)}, \sigma_{j_2}^{(t)})
\end{align}
Note that self-interactions are allowed in this expansion. The $A$ is constructed in a fashion similar to ACE. $B$ terms are constructed from $A$ terms by forming tensor products of the features and then symmetrizing , also as done in the ACE archithecture.
