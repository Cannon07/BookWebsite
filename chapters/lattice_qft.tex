\chapter{Lattice Quantum Field Theory}
\label{chap:lattice_qft}
\noindent\rule{11cm}{0.4pt} \\
\textbf{Prerequisites:} \autoref{chap:qft_basics}  \\
\textbf{Difficulty Level:} *** \\
\noindent\rule{11cm}{0.4pt}
\vspace{5mm}

Lattice quantum field theories study field theories defined on a lattice. Lattice quantum field theories have the powerful advantage that they describe mathematicaly well-defined objects (unlike most standard quantum field theories), but with the major disadvantage that it is not know if solutions to a lattice quantum field theory converge to the correct continuum solution in all cases.
